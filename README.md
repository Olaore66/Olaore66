## 👨‍💻 About Me

Full-stack software engineer with **4+ years** shipping production **mobile banking and payment systems** across African markets. I build the whole product: **Flutter** apps up front, **Spring Boot** and **Node.js** services underneath, and the ledgers that make money move safely.

I've spent thousands of hours building, shipping and debugging production applications. I understand how systems work, and how they break, and I take ownership of every product like it's my own.

- 🏦 Architected mobile banking apps for **tier-1 banks in Burkina Faso, Ethiopia and The Gambia**
- 🔌 Turned **Temenos T24** core banking APIs into fast, mobile-ready Experience APIs
- 💸 Building **Wisemonie** end to end: Flutter app, Spring Boot backend, PostgreSQL and BaaS
- ⚡ Building real-time backends for streaming and community platforms (Socket.io, Redis, LiveKit)
- 🎓 B.Eng. Computer Engineering, Federal University of Technology, Minna
